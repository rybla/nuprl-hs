Regular sets

Union, concatenation, and star as predicates on words; Kleene identities.

Constable et al. §11.4

Regular sets and Kleene algebra, following Constable et al. §11.4.

Words are lists over an alphabet `E = Atom`. A “regular set” is a predicate `E* → U{i}` (a subset of `E*`). Union `r + s`, concatenation `r s`, and star `r*` are defined as in the book; containment and equality of sets are pointwise. The identities below are true of *all* subsets, not only the regular ones (the book’s observation after Figure 11.8).

-- | Regular sets and Kleene algebra, following Constable et al. §11.4.
--
-- Words are lists over an alphabet `E = Atom`. A “regular set” is a
-- predicate `E* → U{i}` (a subset of `E*`). Union `r + s`, concatenation
-- `r s`, and star `r*` are defined as in the book; containment and equality
-- of sets are pointwise. The identities below are true of *all* subsets,
-- not only the regular ones (the book’s observation after Figure 11.8).
theory Regular

comment book "Constable et al. §11.4: regular sets."

abs E == Atom
abs Estar == List Atom
abs RE == Estar → U{i}

abs Append(xs; ys) == list_ind xs (ys) (h, t, ih. h :: ih)
abs Flatten(xss) == list_ind xss ([]) (h, t, ih. Append(h; ih))
abs AllIn(r; ys) == list_ind ys True (h, t, v. r h ∧ v)

abs Contained(r; s) == ∀x:Estar. r x → s x
abs Req(r; s) == Contained(r; s) ∧ Contained(s; r)

abs Plus(r; s) == λx. r x ∨ s x
abs Concat(r; s) == λx. ∃u:Estar. ∃v:Estar. Append(u; v) = x ∈ Estar ∧ r u ∧ s v
abs Star(r) ==
  λx. ∃ys:List Estar. AllIn(r; ys) ∧ Flatten(ys) = x ∈ Estar
abs EmptyR == λx. False
abs Epsilon == λx. x = [] ∈ Estar
abs Singleton(a) == λx. x = (a :: []) ∈ Estar

soft E, Estar, RE, Append, Flatten, AllIn, Contained, Req
soft Plus, Concat, Star, EmptyR, Epsilon, Singleton
