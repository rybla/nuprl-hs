theory CPS

CPS / double negation

Continuations as ¬¬; Kolmogorov translation; irrefutability of excluded middle.

Continuation-passing style as a double-negation translation.

`Cont(A; R) = (A → R) → R` is the type of computations of `A` in continuation-passing style with answer type `R`. Taking `R = False` recovers double negation: `Cont(A; False) = ¬¬A`.

Return and bind are the continuation monad. The Kolmogorov / Gödel–Gentzen translation of classical logic is this CPS transform at `R = False`: `A → ¬¬A` is return, and `¬¬¬A → ¬A` is the constructive triple-negation law. Full DNE `¬¬A → A` is the classical `call/cc` (Peirce), and is not derived. Irrefutability of excluded middle *is* constructive.

-- | Continuation-passing style as a double-negation translation.
--
-- `Cont(A; R) = (A → R) → R` is the type of computations of `A` in
-- continuation-passing style with answer type `R`. Taking `R = False`
-- recovers double negation: `Cont(A; False) = ¬¬A`.
--
-- Return and bind are the continuation monad. The Kolmogorov / Gödel–Gentzen
-- translation of classical logic is this CPS transform at `R = False`:
-- `A → ¬¬A` is return, and `¬¬¬A → ¬A` is the constructive triple-negation
-- law. Full DNE `¬¬A → A` is the classical `call/cc` (Peirce), and is not
-- derived. Irrefutability of excluded middle *is* constructive.
theory CPS

abs DN(A) == ¬¬A
abs Cont(A; R) == (A → R) → R
abs Stable(A) == ¬¬A → A
